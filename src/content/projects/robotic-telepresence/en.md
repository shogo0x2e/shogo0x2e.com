Robotic Telepresence explores embodied communication through a group of modular robots and projection. The system investigates how a remote speaker’s physical presence can be represented across multiple moving bodies.

## Contribution

- System development in Unity
- Experiment design and implementation
- Evaluation and paper writing

## Publication

Shogo Kitada and Kazuki Takashima. “Evaluation of a Robot-Group Telepresence System Representing a Remote Speaker’s Embodiment.” IPSJ SIG HCI Technical Report, 2026-HCI-217, no. 6, pp. 1–7, 2026. [in Japanese]
