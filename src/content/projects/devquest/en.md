DEVQUEST helps students deepen their self-understanding while drafting Japanese entry sheets.An LLM asks follow-up questions so that the writing process begins with reflection rather than a blank form.

## Contribution

- Implemented backend features with Prisma and tRPC.
- Worked across the Next.js application and data model.
- Used Clerk,PlanetScale,and the OpenAI API.

## Outcome

The project received the LayerX Award at a GEEK CAMP hackathon.
