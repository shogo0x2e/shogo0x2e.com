At the request of Minato City Shibahama Elementary School, ShibaLab collaborated with students in its Advanced Technology Club to create a projection-mapping work.

As a team, we planned a workshop that enabled the students to learn about emerging technologies while participating in the production process. The completed work, “Shibahama,” was presented to the entire school on March 7, 2023.
