Black Hole Boogie is a single-player VR space-action game created in Sweden by a multinational team. During a 90-second mission, players rescue astronauts and defend them from asteroids and other hazards around a black hole.

## Interaction and implementation

- Hand-tracked slap, punch, grab, and gun gestures using Meta XR SDK.
- A zero-gravity environment with randomized astronauts, asteroids, and aliens.
- Shader and VFX Graph effects for the black hole and weapon interactions.
- Spatial audio that responds to the player’s position and orientation.

## Constraints

The team adapted interactions for differences in player height and limb length. Visual effects also had to fit within Meta Quest 3 performance limits, and the gun gesture required iteration because hand occlusion reduced tracking reliability.

Working across cultures and disciplines made the development process as important as the final artifact.
