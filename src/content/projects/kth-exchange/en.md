As part of Shibaura Institute of Technology’s International Program, I studied at KTH Royal Institute of Technology from August 2024 to January 2025. My main goals were to explore Human-Computer Interaction and experience participatory teaching in Sweden.

In Advanced Graphics and Interaction, I assembled a multinational team and led the development of VR projects. User Experience Design and Evaluation combined paper seminars, workshops, critical writing, and peer review, allowing me to understand UX through discussion and practice.

Working entirely in English involved leading a team, presenting our work, and coordinating with exhibition staff. I often had to ask again or explain the same idea in a different way. That process gave me confidence that I could live independently and move creative work forward in an international environment.
