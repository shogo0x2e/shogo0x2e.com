Boombloom is an interactive installation that launches projected fireworks wherever a thrown ball hits the wall. Hitting a shooting-star target changes the firework pattern, adding a game-like layer to an otherwise immediate concept: throw a ball and see the wall respond.

I developed the sensing system that detects where each ball hits the wall using a HOKUYO LiDAR sensor.

The photograph and short video document the work in operation on October 31, 2025.
