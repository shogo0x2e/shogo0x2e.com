NginLink lets engineers exchange a compact set of links to their technical profiles and use them as prompts for conversation at social events.

## Contribution

- Implemented the interface with Next.js and shadcn/ui.
- Built client-side data-fetching logic.
- Collaborated with a Go,Gin,GORM,and MySQL backend.

## Outcome

The project received an Effort Award at a GEEK CAMP hackathon.
