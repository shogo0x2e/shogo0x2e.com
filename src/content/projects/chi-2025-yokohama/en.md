I attended CHI 2025 in Yokohama to support a senior laboratory member’s presentation. Alongside helping with the demo session, I attended paper sessions and experienced interactive demonstrations presented by researchers from around the world.

The conference introduced me to an unfamiliar and deeply inspiring world, where new questions and forms of interaction were being explored in the same venue. I hope to develop my own research toward presenting at an international conference like CHI.
